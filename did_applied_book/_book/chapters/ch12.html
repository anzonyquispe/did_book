4  Chapter 12: Hollingsworth et al. (2022) — Marijuana Legalization and Opioid Mortality

Research Question: How does marijuana legalization (medical and recreational) affect opioid mortality?

Data: State-year panel 1999–2018. Two binary treatments: med_cannabis (medical legalization) and rec_cannabis (recreational legalization). Outcome: logmortality (log opioid mortality rate per 100k).
